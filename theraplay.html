Understanding Theraplay

A playful approach to emotional and relational well-being.

Theraplay is an evidence-based therapeutic intervention for children ages 0–12 who experience emotional or behavioral challenges and their caregivers.

The approach focuses on the relationship between a child and their caregiver, using guided interactions to help build feelings of safety, care, and connection.

Sometimes connection is strengthened through moments of shared play, comfort, and delight.

When families seek support

When might Theraplay help?

Families may explore Theraplay when children are having difficulty with emotions, behavior, or their relationships with caregivers. Situations described by Milestone include:

01 / Difficult experiences

Rebuilding a sense of security

Disruptions in a child's relationship with a significant caregiver following violence in the home or community, caregiver substance use, sexual abuse, divorce, separation, or loss.

02 / Transitions

Navigating changes in care

Changes in placement or caregivers, including experiences related to foster care.

03 / Emotions

Supporting big feelings

Children showing challenging behaviors or struggling to manage their emotions.

04 / Family well-being

Care through health challenges

Physical or mental health difficulties affecting members of the family.

05 / Relationships

Strengthening your bond

Caregivers who would like support in strengthening their relationship with their child.

What sessions involve

Guided play. Shared connection.

In Theraplay, a therapist guides a caregiver and child through playful games, developmentally challenging activities, and nurturing interactions.

These shared experiences are designed to help caregivers respond to and regulate a child's emotions and behavior while communicating care, joy, delight, and safety.

For more information, visit What Is Theraplay? (opens in a new tab) from the Theraplay organization.

The emphasis is on helping children experience safety and connection in the caregiver–child relationship.

The heart of Theraplay

Helping connection feel safe, joyful, and real.

Through these interactions, children can experience care and connection, which may support their overall functioning and relationships.

Caregivers are active participants in this process, sharing experiences that are centered on the relationship.

What is the goal?

To create opportunities for children and caregivers to experience warmth, understanding, and connection through guided interactions.

Every family's needs and experiences are different.
