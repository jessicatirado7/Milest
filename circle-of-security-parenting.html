Understanding COS-P

Building security through connection.

Circle of Security Parenting (COS-P) is an evidence-based intervention for caregivers of children ages 0–12, grounded in decades of research about supporting secure caregiver–child relationships.

Milestone’s therapists use this approach to help parents and caregivers better recognize their children’s emotional needs, respond thoughtfully, and strengthen their relationships.

Understand your child’s emotional world. Support the relationship that helps them grow.

How we support caregivers

What can caregivers explore through COS-P?

The program focuses on helping caregivers reflect on their children’s needs and respond in ways that support emotional security.

01 / Understanding

Recognize emotional needs

Learn to better read a child’s emotional cues and understand what they may be communicating.

02 / Regulation

Support emotional development

Help children manage their emotions and turn to caregivers for support when they need it.

03 / Confidence

Strengthen self-esteem

Encourage a child’s developing sense of self through secure, supportive relationships.

04 / Connection

Trust your caregiving instincts

Honor the wisdom and desire caregivers already bring to helping their children feel secure.

Program options

Support that meets families where they are.

COS-P can be offered within individual or family psychotherapy. Milestone has also periodically offered caregiver groups.

Milestone’s therapists are described as certified COS-P facilitators. Contact the practice to learn about current providers, program formats, and availability.
