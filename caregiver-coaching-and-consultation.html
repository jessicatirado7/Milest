Understanding through reflection

New understanding can shape new responses.

We offer reflective mental health consultation to parents, foster parents, respite providers, childcare providers, and teachers caring for a child with emotional or behavioral challenges.

This consultation supports caregivers in understanding and responding to a child's mental health needs. Reflecting on the child's needs and the relationships involved can help bring new ways of responding to difficult emotions or behaviors into focus.

Space to reflect on what's happening, understand the relationship, and discover new ways forward.

Our approach

Reflective consultation and practical coaching.

Our therapists offer two complementary approaches to supporting caregivers as they navigate challenging emotions, behaviors, and interactions with children.

01 / Consultation

Reflective mental health consultation

Consultation helps caregivers develop a clearer understanding of a child's emotional and behavioral needs, including the relational dynamics that may be influencing an interaction. Through reflection, new ways of responding may become more apparent.

02 / Coaching

In-the-moment caregiver coaching

Our therapists also coach caregivers during caregiver-child interactions. This helps caregivers put new insights about a child's behavior and relationships into practice while developing parenting skills addressed in therapy or consultation.

Who we support

Care for the caregivers, too.

Supporting a child also means supporting the people involved in their everyday care. Caregiver Coaching & Consultation offers room to reflect, build understanding, and practice new responses.

Caregiver support includes

  • Parents
  • Foster parents
  • Respite providers
  • Childcare providers
  • Teachers
