Our clinicians can help determine which type of care may be appropriate for you or your family.

Mental Health Diagnostic Assessments

We offer mental health diagnostic assessments to individuals of all ages to determine a diagnosis and provide recommendations for mental health care.

When working with young children (ages 0–5), our therapists have specialized training and experience in the assessment and diagnosis of mental health concerns that may occur during early childhood. To appropriately assess young children, our providers utilize a separate diagnostic manual created for this population. The diagnostic assessment process for children in this stage of development includes a caregiver-only clinical interview, observation of caregiver-child interactions and, whenever possible, an observation of the child in another context such as a childcare or preschool setting.

Neuropsychological Evaluations for Children & Adolescents

We provide neuropsychological evaluations for children and teens when more information is required to understand cognitive, social, emotional, and relational functioning, incorporating knowledge about the childhood and adolescent stage of development.

These evaluations are often sought when there is a desire for diagnostic clarity for mental health concerns or to determine the existence of neurocognitive concerns such as ADHD, Autism Spectrum Disorder, or Learning Disorders.

Consultation & Coordination of Care

We support coordinated mental health care for clients of all ages. With your permission, we collaborate with other providers involved in your care, including medical providers, medication prescribers, occupational and speech therapists, other mental health professionals, case managers, and skills workers. This collaboration helps align treatment goals, share relevant information, and support continuity of care.

When helpful and desired, we also consult with family members, caregivers, teachers, childcare providers, and other support people. Through consultation, we help those involved in your daily life better understand your mental health needs and develop supportive, consistent ways to respond.

Psychotherapy for Individuals, Couples & Families

We provide psychotherapy for children, adolescents, and adults, as well as couples and families. Our approach is tailored to each client’s needs, stage of life, and goals, with attention to how relationships and life experiences influence emotional well-being.

Our relational services help couples and families strengthen connection, improve communication, navigate conflict and transitions, and address patterns that affect their relationships and mental health.

For young children, therapy includes the direct or close involvement of parents or caregivers. When working with children and adolescents, we partner with caregivers whenever appropriate to support their understanding of their child’s mental health needs and strengthen their ability to respond in helpful, supportive ways.

Virtual Mental Health Care

We offer virtual therapy through a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform, allowing you to attend sessions from a private, comfortable space. Virtual sessions are available for individual, couples, and family therapy when appropriate for your needs. Your clinician can help determine whether virtual care is a good fit. Availability depends on your location at the time of the session and your clinician’s licensing requirements.

Group Therapy

We periodically offer therapeutic and peer support groups that provide opportunities to connect with others, explore shared experiences, and develop skills to support mental health and well-being. Each group is designed for a specific age range, topic, or area of need.

Groups are not currently being offered.

Please check back for future offerings, or contact us to share your interest in a particular type of group. We welcome your ideas and requests!

Specializations

Every experience is different. Explore some of the ways our clinicians support children, adults, couples, and families.

Stress, Life Changes & Trauma

Difficult experiences and significant life changes can affect how we feel, relate to others, and navigate daily life. We support children, adolescents, adults, couples, and families experiencing emotional or relational challenges related to stress, loss, change, trauma, or other adverse experiences.

Therapy offers space to make sense of these experiences, build coping skills, strengthen relationships, and work toward greater safety, stability, and well-being. We tailor treatment to each client’s needs, experiences, and goals.

Our therapists are trained in a range of evidence-based approaches to support healing and growth. Depending on your needs and your therapist’s training, treatment may include:

  • Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing (EMDR)
  • Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT)
  • Trauma-Informed Child-Parent Psychotherapy (TI-CPP; CPP)
  • Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)

Child & Adolescent Mental Health

Children and teens may express emotional distress through changes in their mood, behavior, relationships, or ability to manage everyday demands. We support young people experiencing challenges such as anxiety, difficulty managing emotions, behavioral concerns, school difficulties, reactions to stressors, and conflict with family members or peers.

Our therapists tailor care to each child’s developmental stage, strengths, and needs. Through developmentally appropriate approaches, we help children and teens understand and express their feelings, build coping skills, and strengthen their relationships.

We also partner with parents and caregivers to better understand what may be contributing to these challenges and develop practical ways to respond. Caregiver involvement is tailored to the young person’s age and needs, with attention to supporting trust, growing independence, and connection within the family.

Our therapists draw on a range of therapeutic approaches to support children, teens, and their families. Depending on your family’s needs and your therapist’s training, services may include:

  • Circle of Security Parenting (COS-P)
  • Parent-Child Interaction Therapy
  • Play Therapy
  • Caregiver Coaching & Consultation
  • Social-Emotional Skill Building
  • Sand Tray Therapy
  • Art Therapy
  • Theraplay
  • Trauma-Informed Child-Parent Psychotherapy (TI-CPP)
  • Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT)

Attachment & Relationships

Our relationships shape how we experience connection, trust, and emotional safety throughout life. We support children, adolescents, adults, couples, and families who are navigating relationship challenges, disruptions in close relationships, or patterns that make it difficult to feel understood and connected.

Therapy can help clients explore how past and present relationship experiences influence their emotions, expectations, and interactions with others. Together, we work toward recognizing needs, improving communication, establishing healthy boundaries, navigating conflict, and strengthening connection.

This work may take place in individual, couples, or family therapy. Whether you are exploring your own relationship patterns, strengthening a caregiver-child relationship, or addressing challenges with a partner or family member, we tailor treatment to your needs and goals.

Our therapists are trained in a range of relational approaches. Depending on your needs and your therapist’s training, treatment may include:

  • Theraplay
  • Circle of Security Parenting (COS-P)
  • Couple’s Therapy
  • Family Therapy

Perinatal Mental Health

Did you know, 1 in 7 women and 1 in 10 dads suffer from postpartum depression?

Caring for the mental health of childbearing families during the perinatal period (pregnancy and postpartum) is specialized work that our therapists are passionate about. With a certified perinatal mental health professional on staff, we provide care to families that is knowledgeable and resourceful through this important life milestone.

Scheduling Your First Visit

We're happy to help you schedule an initial appointment or coordinate a 10–15 minute phone consultation with one of our therapists to answer your questions. Call or email us to get started.

Call 651-500-2389 → Email our team →

For privacy, please avoid including sensitive health information in a regular email.

What happens next?

  1. Receive your portal link. Once your intake appointment is scheduled, we'll email you a link to your Secure Client Portal.
  2. Complete your paperwork. You'll complete intake, policy, and financial forms through the portal. Allow approximately 15–20 minutes. Appointment reminders may arrive by email, text, or both.
  3. Prepare for your appointment. Please complete all required forms at least 24 hours before your first visit.

Scheduling & Cancellation Policies

  • If the required intake paperwork is not completed at least 24 hours before your intake appointment, your therapist may cancel and reschedule it until the paperwork is complete.
  • Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice incur a $75 cancellation fee.
  • If you miss an appointment without notice, the full fee for the scheduled service is required before future appointments can be scheduled. This fee cannot be billed to insurance.
  • All fees and copayments are due at the time of service.

Get to know your clinician

Victoria Tinucci

MA

I am a mental health practitioner completing the required post-graduate supervised experience to become a Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist.

I firmly believe that we do not grow when we stay in our comfort zones. Growth can be uncomfortable, and you do not need to navigate it alone. I am here to walk alongside you through life’s curveballs, providing a safe, collaborative environment where you can show up authentically as yourself. My goal is to meet you where you are, honoring your unique experiences while supporting you through making and maintaining lasting change.

Trained as a marriage and family therapist, I use a systemic lens to understand the whole person sitting in front of me, not just a singular aspect of who you are. I take a strength-based approach to therapy, believing that every person has unique experiences, abilities, and resilience that form the foundations for growth. Together, we will build on those strengths and identify what is no longer serving you so you can make lasting, meaningful change.

Others describe me as warm, kind, welcoming, and silly. Outside of my passion for the mental health field, I enjoy reading, creating, and spending time with my family, pets, and friends. You will often find me with an iced coffee or some caffeinated beverage in hand.

My previous clinical experience involves community mental health and early childhood autism day treatment. In these settings, I had the opportunity to work with individuals and their families of all ages, navigating crisis and challenges associated with their or a family member’s symptomatology.

I currently provide telehealth and in-office appointments for individuals, couples, and families of all ages. Topics of specialty include grief and loss, anxiety, depression, self-esteem, trauma, life transitions, and personal growth and exploration. It would be an honor to walk with you through this journey and create a space for you to become the best version of yourself.

Education:

  • Master of Arts, Marriage & Family Therapy - Saint Mary's University of Minnesota
  • Bachelor of Science, Youth Studies- University of Minnesota Twin Cities

Get to know your clinician

Aaron Elliott

MSW, LGSW, LADC

My name is Aaron Elliott, and I am a mental health practitioner and addiction counselor offering services both in-person and virtually. I am dedicated to helping adolescents and adults navigate life's most difficult challenges, including addiction, depression, anxiety, ADHD, social anxiety, self-esteem issues, and relationship difficulties.

My therapeutic approach is highly customized to meet you where you are. I provide therapy for both individuals and couples. I blend evidence-based practices—such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), and Exposure Therapy (Systematic Desensitization)—with practical grounding techniques and specialized addiction counseling. My greatest joy as a clinician is supporting my clients as they achieve their goals and watching them grow into their fullest potential.

My drive to help others is deeply personal; my own life, family, and community have been profoundly shaped by mental health and addiction struggles. This lived experience fuels my commitment to providing compassionate, non-judgmental care. Please feel free to reach out if you would like more information or to see how we can work together.

Education:

  • Master of Social Work (MSW) – Saint Mary’s University of Minnesota
  • Licensed Alcohol & Drug Counselor (LADC) Training – Metropolitan State University
  • Bachelor of Social Work (BSW) – Winona State University

License & Credentials

  • Licensed Alcohol & Drug Counselor – MN Board of Behavioral Health & Therapy – License #305149
  • Licensed Graduate Social Worker – MN Board of Social Work – License #22852
