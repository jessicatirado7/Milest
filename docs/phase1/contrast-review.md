## Proposed visual changes

1. Replace the blue cards' 8% white overlay with a 4% black tint. This darkens only the card surface. The surrounding `#526f7f` section blue, text colors, borders, layout, typography and photographs stay the same. Effective card background changes from approximately `#607b89` to `#4f6b7a`.
2. Make inline resource links in blue-section body paragraphs white and underlined. This addresses the missing `.text-white` utility behavior on PCIT/EMDR without restoring unrelated Bootstrap rules or changing global link colors.
3. Use a white, offset keyboard-focus outline for links within these blue sections, since the general blue focus outline blends into the blue background. Normal and hover link text remain white; the focus outline appears only during keyboard focus.

Exact proposal: [contrast-proposal.css](contrast-proposal.css). If approved, incorporate these rules into the existing `css/milestone-pages.css`; do not add the review stylesheet as another site dependency.

| Element | Current measured ratio | Proposed calculated ratio* | Required normal-text minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pale card text `#f7f9f8` | 4.23:1 | 5.34:1 | 4.5:1 |
| White card text/links | 4.47:1 | 5.65:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Pale Careers labels `#eaf1f4` | 3.91:1 | 4.95:1 | 4.5:1 |
| PCIT/EMDR resource links on section blue | 1.23:1 | 5.33:1 | 4.5:1 |

*Proposed ratios use the rounded effective RGB card surface `#4f6b7a`, calculated using WCAG relative luminance. Alpha compositing and tool rounding can produce small differences. Browser axe results are recorded separately; ratios are not a whole-site conformance claim. The new white focus outline contrasts with the section blue at approximately 5.33:1 and the card surface at 5.65:1.

Primary criterion: [WCAG 2.1 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#contrast-minimum); focus treatment also supports [1.4.11 Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#non-text-contrast) and [2.4.7 Focus Visible](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#focus-visible).

## Verification

Evidence is stored in [contrast-checks.json](evidence/contrast-checks.json), generated by [check-contrast.cjs](check-contrast.cjs). Each root page is checked at 390×844 and 1440×1000, first using current styles and then the same page with the draft CSS injected in memory. The check records full axe violations, incomplete checks, computed colors and JavaScript errors. Resource-link normal/hover/focus/active states are checked separately. Visited-link behavior is specified in CSS but is not claimed as browser-verified, due to browser history privacy protections.

Automated scans and these screenshots do not replace the outstanding screen-reader, true browser zoom, PDF and hosted-form tests. Approval here accepts the appearance of this contrast proposal, not a claim of full WCAG conformance.

Verified result: 14 pages × 2 viewports × current/proposed = **56 axe scans**. Current styles produced contrast violations on 11 pages at both widths; draft-injected pages produced **zero violations under the selected WCAG tags** at both widths. No JavaScript exceptions were recorded. PCIT/EMDR resource links remained white and underlined in the normal, hover, focus and active checks; keyboard focus computed a 3px white outline with a 4px offset. The preview itself was also checked for isolated current/proposed styling across all 11 page choices. This does not include the outstanding full manual Phase 1 verification.
