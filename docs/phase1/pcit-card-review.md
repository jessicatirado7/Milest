# PCIT card appearance — follow-up review

**Selected and implemented: ivory with dark blue text.** The owner approved this option. The applied PCIT card uses `#f7f5ef` with `#283c46` text and a matching dark keyboard-focus outline. Rules are integrated into `css/milestone-pages.css`; no review stylesheet is loaded by the site. The original alternatives below are retained as review history.

The owner approved the initial contrast proposal, with an exception for the “Who PCIT supports” card: the darker proposed card blended too closely into the surrounding blue. The owner suggested ivory with dark blue text and asked for alternatives.

**Recommendation: ivory with dark blue text.** It gives the strongest separation and uses the existing warm ivory/ink palette. Scope is this PCIT card only; no clinical wording or layout change.

[Open the interactive card review](http://127.0.0.1:4173/docs/phase1/pcit-card-options.html) · [actual-card comparison image](evidence/pcit-card-options.png)

| Option | Background | Text | Calculated text contrast | Visual tradeoff |

| Muted blue with white text | `#5b7583` | `#ffffff` | 4.86:1 | Closest to original; slightly deepen its tint and brighten all card text |
| Original reference | effective `#607b89` | mixed pale/white | body 4.23:1, link 4.47:1 | Visual reference; normal-text failures remain pending the chosen fix |

The 4.5:1 normal-text minimum applies to these text combinations. Ratios use WCAG relative luminance and are rounded. Keeping the exact original surface with the current pale text would retain the confirmed contrast failure. Putting the existing dark ink on that same medium-blue surface would produce only about 2.57:1, so an ivory or pale-blue surface is needed for that ink choice. No new brand color system is introduced.

## Validation

[Browser evidence](evidence/pcit-options-checks.json) is generated by [pcit-card-options.cjs](pcit-card-options.cjs). It scans application pages at 390 and 1440 CSS pixels after approved changes, then scans each PCIT candidate and the original reference at both widths, recording computed heading/body/link colors, focus and hover states. Original PCIT contrast failures are expected until a candidate is selected; they are not silently marked resolved. All-page scans are separate from the in-memory candidate scans.

Verified: 28 applied-page scans, with only the pending PCIT card producing contrast violations. The other 13 root pages had no violations under the selected WCAG tags at either width. The 8 candidate/reference scans confirmed that all three alternative cards have no detected violations at either width; the original reference retains its expected contrast failures. Candidate heading/body/link colors, underlined hover links and appropriate dark/white keyboard-focus outlines were confirmed. No JavaScript exceptions were recorded during candidate checks.

Applied-source verification: [pcit-ivory-applied-checks.json](evidence/pcit-ivory-applied-checks.json) checks 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS px with **no injected candidate CSS**. It verifies the ivory surface, dark text, unchanged section blue, hover/keyboard focus, document width, console exceptions and selected axe WCAG rules. Applied screenshots: [390px](evidence/pcit-ivory-applied-390.png) and [1440px](evidence/pcit-ivory-applied-1440.png).
