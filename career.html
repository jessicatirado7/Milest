Why Milestone

A supportive place to build your practice.

Milestone Mental Health is a group private practice providing specialized mental health care for children, adolescents, adults, and families. As our community's need for care grows, we are seeking providers who want to make a meaningful difference.

Our clinic includes four fully equipped play therapy offices, an observation and coaching therapy room with a one-way window and listening equipment, and individual offices.

Room to grow, with the space and support to focus on meaningful care.

Employment opportunities

Find your place at Milestone.

Explore the position details below. Please contact the clinic to confirm current openings and availability.

Outpatient therapy · Independent contractor

Mental Health Professional or Practitioner

We are seeking mental health professionals or pre-licensed mental health practitioners to provide outpatient services in Oakdale, Minnesota. LPCC, LMFT, LICSW, LADC, or LP credentials are preferred; providers working toward licensure are also encouraged to inquire.

01 / Responsibilities

What you'll do

  • Provide outpatient individual and family therapy.
  • Conduct diagnostic intake assessments.
  • Develop comprehensive treatment plans.
  • Coordinate client care with other providers.
  • Maintain client documentation and treatment notes.

02 / Experience

Preferred experience & interests

  • Experience providing outpatient services; at least one year licensed is preferred, but pre-licensed applicants are welcome.
  • EMDR training or certification, Brainspotting, or Somatic Experiencing.
  • Registered Play Therapist (RPT), or supervision by an RPT-S.
  • Working with children and families, or early childhood (DC 0–5).
  • An interest in developing one of these specializations; professional development support may be available.

Scheduling arrangements

Ways to join the team.

01 / Full-time

Full-time opportunity

One provider comfortable working with adolescents and adults. Up to four days per week, with a caseload of approximately 20–24 clients weekly.

02 / Part-time

Part-time opportunities

Two providers comfortable working with children and adolescents. Up to two or three days per week, with a caseload of approximately 12–18 clients weekly per provider.

Practice support

More support for the work that matters.

Milestone describes the following resources and benefits for its independent contractors, helping providers focus on client care and the growth of their practices.

01 / Compensation

Competitive fee split

A fee split based on monthly revenue, described as up to 70/30.

02 / Operations

Operational support

Referral sources, EHR, insurance credentialing and contracting, billing, balance collection, and MN Care Tax.

03 / Flexibility

Flexible, furnished spaces

Set your own schedule and work from a furnished office or play therapy room.

04 / Resources

Marketing & communications

Advertising and marketing support, plus HIPAA-compliant phone and email systems.

05 / Community

Professional community

Peer consultation, professional collaboration, and possible supervision options for pre-licensed providers.

06 / Development

Continued growth

A sign-on bonus and a stipend for continuing professional development are described among the opportunities.
